__About Me__<br>
I am now an Assistant Professor with Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). I obtained my Ph.D. degree from The University of Hong Kong in 2018, and the B.Eng. degree from University of Science and Technology of China in 2014. I was a postdoc research fellow with National University of Singapore in 2018-2021.
